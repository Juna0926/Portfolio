Data-Efficient Stoma Lesion Localization via WSOL and Pseudo-Label Refinement with Domain Knowledge

Research
Feb. 2026 — Jun. 2026

Machine Learning and Data Mining Laboratory · Collaborative Research

Setting 15 bounding-box-labeled images와 707 unlabeled images의 limited-annotation setting을 다뤘습니다.

Method WSOL로 candidate lesion regions를 생성하고 red-color prominence, luminance contrast, orientation, center proximity 등 stoma-specific domain knowledge로 pseudo-label을 refine했습니다.

Result 673 / 707 candidates를 유지해 Faster R-CNN을 재학습했으며 mean IoU 0.7796, Dice 0.8681을 기록했습니다.
