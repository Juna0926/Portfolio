DIGITAL HEALTH · LONGITUDINAL MENTAL HEALTH

Digital Phenotype-Based Mental Health Prediction and Derived Feature Contribution Analysis

May. 2025 — Jun. 2026Yongin Severance Hospital Digital Healthcare Hackathon → 23rd Avison Biomedical Symposium 2026

Digital phenotype와 EMA longitudinal data를 이용해 PHQ-9와 GAD-7을 예측하는 LSTM-based framework를 구축하고, 모델 결과를 personalized monitoring and reporting prototype으로 연결한 연구입니다.

0.6312PHQ-9 R²
0.6159GAD-7 R²
Grand PrizeYongin Severance Hospital Digital Healthcare Hackathon

Longitudinal framework

Digital phenotype와 EMA data를 participant-level longitudinal sequence로 구성해 LSTM-based prediction framework를 구축했습니다.

Prediction targets

PHQ-9와 GAD-7을 예측했으며 각각 R² = 0.6312, 0.6159을 기록했습니다.

Contribution analysis

Derived feature contribution을 분석해 behavioral and contextual features가 mental-health prediction에 어떻게 기여하는지 해석했습니다.

Prototype

Prediction 결과를 continuous mental-health assessment를 위한 personalized monitoring and reporting prototype으로 구현했습니다.

Conference presentation

본 연구는 23rd Avison Biomedical Symposium 2026 · Digital Medicine in Mental Health에서 발표했습니다.
